# Explore questionnaire items across studies and cycles

Search, review, compare, and export questionnaire item metadata from IEA studies.

## About the Item Bank Explorer

Explore individual questionnaire items and their versions, browse by topic, inspect derived variables and construct scales, or review available instruments. Open Details to see wording, response options, administration information, and composition over time.

## Quick guide

1. Choose **Items**, **Derived Variables**, **Topics**, or **Instruments**.
2. Use the sidebar filters and keyword search to narrow the results.
3. Open **Details** to examine item versions and available metadata.
4. Add relevant items or versions to **Export Selection**.
5. Review your cart, remove any unwanted versions, and download Excel.

For more details visit **Tutorial** and **Glossary**.

## Alpha version

IEA Item Bank Explorer is under development. The available data currently focus on ICILS questionnaire content. Additional studies and cycles may be added.

## Reproduction of IEA assessment materials

Reproducing, translating, or redistributing IEA assessment content may require prior written authorization. Consult the IEA study materials guidance before reuse.
